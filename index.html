How to use this

Find someone. Search a handle or name up top, or open the Directory. Enter flies you to them.

Explore connections. Click any dot: their connections light up with names. Click more dots to stack them — each stays until you clear it. The chips under the search box are your active stack: × drops one, Clear all drops them all.

Spot surprises. Bridges lists the longest links between far-apart parts of the map — people connecting groups that otherwise never touch. Click a row to visit it.

Walk the network. A person's panel lists who follows them and who they follow — click a name to hop there. The trail under the search box is your click history; click a name to jump back.

Move around. Drag to pan (hold Shift to drag over dots), scroll to zoom, F for fullscreen, Esc clears everything.
